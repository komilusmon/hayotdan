Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hayotdan Hayotinggacha Android application handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The Hayotdan Hayotinggacha application is designed to work offline and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The application does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information such as names, email addresses, telephone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or financial information.

2. Internet and Online Services

The application is designed as an offline-first poetry reader. It does not require an internet connection to read the included book content. The application does not use an online account system or require users to submit personal information to use its core features.

3. Bookmarks and Reading Preferences

The application may keep reading-related preferences or session data, such as the selected font size, theme, bookmarks, or reading position, so that the reading experience can function properly. Such information is used only for the application's local functionality and is not intended to be transmitted to us.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The application does not intentionally include advertising or third-party analytics services in its current version.

5. Sharing of Information

Because the application does not intentionally collect personal information, it does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

The application does not intentionally collect personal information from children or adults. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the application unintentionally, please contact us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the application and its locally stored data secure. However, no software or device can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Hayotdan Hayotinggacha application, you can contact the app publisher through the contact information provided on the official ANFKA website.
